# T1585.001 - Establish Accounts: Social Media Accounts

- **Tactic(s):** Resource Development
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1585/001/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Adversaries may create and cultivate social media accounts that can be used during targeting.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Resource Development technique: it happens on infrastructure or accounts the adversary controls, outside your logs.

## How to detect it
- Monitor social media for profiles impersonating your organisation or its staff (brand protection services) and report them.
- Train staff to verify unexpected contact requests from 'recruiters' and 'colleagues'.

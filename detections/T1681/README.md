# T1681 - Search Threat Vendor Data

- **Tactic(s):** Reconnaissance
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1681/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Threat actors may seek information/indicators from closed or open threat intelligence sources gathered about their own campaigns, as well as those conducted by other adversaries that may align with their target industries, capabilities/objectives, or other operational concerns.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Reconnaissance technique: it uses public sources or the adversary's own systems, so it leaves little or nothing in your logs.

## How to detect it
- Threat intelligence reports are public or paid; attackers reading them leaves nothing in your environment. Assume published indicators will change.

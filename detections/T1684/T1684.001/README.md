# T1684.001 - Social Engineering: Impersonation

- **Tactic(s):** Stealth
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1684/001/
- **Platforms:** Linux, macOS, Office Suite, SaaS, Windows

## Summary
Adversaries may impersonate a trusted person or organization in order to persuade and trick a target into performing some action on their behalf.

## Status: guidance only
Impersonation of trusted people or organisations happens in mail, chat, phone and video calls.

## How to detect it
- Mail security display-name impersonation rules and external-sender tags; verification procedures for requests involving money, credentials or access.

# T1588.007 - Obtain Capabilities: Artificial Intelligence

- **Tactic(s):** Resource Development
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1588/007/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Adversaries may obtain access to generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools, such as large language models (LLMs), to aid various techniques during targeting.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Resource Development technique: it happens on infrastructure or accounts the adversary controls, outside your logs.

## How to detect it
- Generative AI makes phishing text, translations and scripts better; it changes the quality of attacks, not the logs you see. Use the same phishing and execution detections, and AI-service usage policies for your own staff.

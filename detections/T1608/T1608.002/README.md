# T1608.002 - Stage Capabilities: Upload Tool

- **Tactic(s):** Resource Development
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1608/002/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Adversaries may upload tools to third-party or adversary controlled infrastructure to make it accessible during targeting.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Resource Development technique: it happens on infrastructure the adversary controls, outside your logs.

## How to detect it
- Threat intelligence for tool hosting locations; downloads of tools to your hosts are covered by the T1105 rules.

# T1591.003 - Gather Victim Org Information: Identify Business Tempo

- **Tactic(s):** Reconnaissance
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1591/003/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Adversaries may gather information about the victim's business tempo that can be used during targeting.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Reconnaissance technique: it uses public sources or the adversary's own systems, so it leaves little or nothing in your logs.

## How to detect it
- Attackers time attacks for holidays, weekends and busy periods: keep monitoring and incident response staffed then, and alert on sign-ins and admin activity outside working hours.

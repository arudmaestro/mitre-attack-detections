# T1591.004 - Gather Victim Org Information: Identify Roles

- **Tactic(s):** Reconnaissance
- **ATT&CK link:** https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1591/004/
- **Platforms:** PRE

## Summary
Adversaries may gather information about identities and roles within the victim organization that can be used during targeting.

## Status: guidance only
This is a Reconnaissance technique: it uses public sources or the adversary's own systems, so it leaves little or nothing in your logs.

## How to detect it
- Reduce what is exposed: review what your public websites, DNS records, job postings and employee profiles reveal, and remove what attackers do not need.
- Extra protection for roles that attackers target (executives, finance, IT admins, HR): phishing-resistant MFA, targeted awareness, and stricter mail filtering.
